## 🎯 Core Themes & Takeaways

1. **"Don't learn to hack — hack to learn."**  
   True security and systems depth comes from diving in, breaking things, diagnosing failures, and fixing them rather than passively memorizing scripts or textbook definitions.
2. **The "College Trap" vs. The Active Builder Path**  
   Why sending 500 cold resumes with a 3.8 GPA falls short compared to a candidate who brings a GitHub commit history, a running Proxmox/Linux homelab, and active community participation.
3. **The Student Superpower**  
   How reaching out for 10 minutes of advice while enrolled opens doors with industry leaders that close once you become just another job applicant.
4. **4-Pillar Semester Playbook:**
   - **Lab:** Spin up Linux, Proxmox, Docker, and hypervisors.
   - **Code:** Automate repetitive workflows in Python and Bash.
   - **Write:** Publish post-mortems and CTF writeups demonstrating how you think.
   - **Connect:** Attend local meetups, conferences, and user groups.
